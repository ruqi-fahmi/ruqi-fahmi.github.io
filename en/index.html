About

I have always seen technology as a tool, not the goal itself.

My foundation is management. That is where I learned to read problems, processes, targets, and how people actually work on the ground. The engineering side I built up slowly, through a route I chose myself: bootcamps, practice, and work that kept asking for more.

It started simply. In 2021 I began looking after a foundation's database with MySQL, phpMyAdmin, and XAMPP. From then on a database was no longer coursework, but something to be kept safe because other people relied on it.

After that I took several bootcamps through 2022 and 2023. HTML, Laravel, JavaScript, React, Next.js, Express, MongoDB, and React Native. I learned the technical side not to collect a stack, but because there were problems I wanted to solve.

In 2023 the two sides began to meet. I won a national Marketing Plan Competition at Universitas Andalas, then also won a Qur'an application design competition and reached its national finals. One from the business side, one from the technology side.

At Telkomsel I started in customer service. I ran Telkomsel and Telkom programmes whose rules were fairly complex, then translated them into conversations customers could follow. Two years in that seat gave me a perspective that is hard to get from documentation: how a programme really runs on the ground.

Now I build the systems that support that work. Systems that turn raw collections data into daily operational decisions, from ingestion and the database through to the dashboard the team uses.

What I care about most is not a dashboard that looks busy. What matters is a correct number. Because one metric with the wrong definition can make the decisions wrong too, without anyone noticing.
